## 📌 Project Description

This Power BI dashboard presents an interactive analysis of coffee sales transactions across three store locations – Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore – during the year **Jan 2024 to Dec 2024**.

It helps track key business metrics such as sales performance, product trends, customer behavior, and store-wise comparison. The dashboard is designed for coffee shop managers and business owners to take data-driven decisions.

---

## 📚 Dataset Overview

The dataset includes 8,000+ transactions with the following key fields:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `date` | Transaction date  
| `datetime` | Timestamp of sale  
| `money` | Order amount in ₹  
| `coffee_name` | Coffee product sold  
| `cash_type` | Payment method (Cash/Card)  
| `order_type` | Dine-in / Takeaway / Online  
| `store_location` | Branch (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore)  
| `rating` | Customer rating (1 to 5)

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total and average sales performance  
- Identify most popular coffee items  
- Understand order type and payment patterns  
- Track sales across time and location  
- Measure customer satisfaction using ratings

---

## 📈 Key Visuals in the Dashboard

- KPI Cards (Total Revenue, Orders, Average Value, Avg Rating)  
- Line Chart – Monthly sales trend  
- Donut Chart – Payment type share  
- Bar Chart – Top-selling coffee types  
- Stacked Bar – Order type comparison  
- Bar Chart – Store-wise revenue  
- Table – Average rating by store

---

## 📊 Tools & Techniques Used

- Power BI Desktop  
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformations)  
- DAX (Calculated Measures)  
- Slicers & Filters for interactivity  
- Custom fiscal year handling (April to March)

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- 💳 Card is the most used payment method (~70%)  
- 🥇 Latte is the highest-selling product  
- 🏙️ Mumbai store leads in total revenue  
- ⭐ Average customer rating is 3.2  
- 📈 Sales trend shows strong weekdays and a dip in December
